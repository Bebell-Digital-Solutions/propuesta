DriveDrop runs locally on your browser. To manage files, deploy a simple script in your Google Account. Your data never leaves your device except directly to your Google Drive.

2

Deploy as Web App

  1. Click Deploy > New deployment.
  2. Select type Web app.
  3. Set "Execute as" to Me.
  4. Set "Who has access" to Anyone.
  5. Click Deploy, authorize access if asked, and copy the Web app URL.
3

Connect DriveDrop

Go back to the Connect URL tab and paste your new Web app URL.
